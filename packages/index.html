Choosing wisely

Analog CCTV or IP CCTV?

Both technologies can be good choices. The right one depends on your property and requirements.

  • Analog CCTV: typically economical for straightforward installations using coaxial cabling and DVRs.
  • IP CCTV: suited to flexible networked deployments using NVRs, Ethernet and PoE equipment.
  • Storage: recording retention depends on drive size, resolution, bitrate, frame rate and recording schedule.
  • Site survey: cable runs and placement affect the final quotation.
